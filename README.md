# Geo AI Explorer

Create a comprehensive and professional 5-hour HTML presentation/workshop titled “A Geologist’s Guide to AI & Machine Learning in Mineral Exploration” for pre-graduate students specializing in Geology and/or Geophysics, assuming little or no previous AI/ML knowledge. Read and use the three Word files in the same folder as reference, but do not follow their structure or limit yourself to their content—use your own expertise and freely add, remove, reorganize, and expand topics. Cover a wide range of AI/ML concepts and real mineral-exploration applications including geology, geochemistry, geophysics, drilling, core logging, remote sensing, geological modelling, QA/QC, prospectivity mapping, targeting, computer vision, data analysis, and AI tools. Include many practical workflows, diagrams, examples, case studies, activities, demonstrations, and end-to-end exploration workflows, especially showing how AI can support exploration decisions while keeping geological reasoning and validation central. Make the content educational and understandable for geology/geophysics students rather than a generic AI course. Create it as a dynamic, animated, visually rich, interactive folder-based HTML presentation, and feel free to search for and download useful online geological images, maps, diagrams, or other visuals when they improve the presentation. You have complete freedom to make the presentation as comprehensive as necessary for a full 5-hour workshop.
